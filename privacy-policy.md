# Cartdown Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

## Summary

Cartdown is a grocery budget app. It has no accounts, no ads and no servers of its own. Your shopping trips stay on your phone.

## What Cartdown stores

Your trips (budget, store name and dates), the items you add (name, price, quantity and weight) and any refunds. This is saved in a database on your device only. The developer cannot see it.

## Camera

Cartdown uses the camera only when you scan a price tag. The photo is read on your phone and deleted right after. It is never uploaded. You can say no to camera access and enter items by hand instead.

## Google ML Kit

Price tags are read with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your phone. The photos and the text read from them are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app version and performance metrics, to keep the service working and improve it. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

Cartdown does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no analytics and no ads.

## Deleting your data

Delete a trip from History at any time. Uninstalling Cartdown, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes everything it saved.

## Children

Cartdown is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with a new effective date.
